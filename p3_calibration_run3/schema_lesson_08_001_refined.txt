A designer is creating two decorative tiles for a mosaic. Both tiles are equilateral triangles. The first tile, ABC, has all angles measuring 60° each. The second tile, DEF, is larger, with all sides measuring 12 cm and all angles also measuring 60° each.

The designer knows that these two equilateral triangles are similar by the Side-Side-Side (SSS) Similarity Theorem, with a scale factor of 1.5 — meaning that each side of triangle DEF is 1.5 times the length of the corresponding side in triangle ABC.

What is the length of side AB in the smaller tile ABC?
