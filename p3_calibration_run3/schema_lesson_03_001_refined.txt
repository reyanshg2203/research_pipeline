To create a smaller version of the logo for a website thumbnail, she reduces the triangle using a dilation centered at point P with a scale factor of 0.6.

After the dilation, the new triangle DEF corresponds to the original triangle ABC, with each side of DEF measuring 0.6 times the length of the corresponding side in ABC.

When the designer measures the side DE in the reduced logo, what length does she find?
