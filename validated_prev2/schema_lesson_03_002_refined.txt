A graphic designer is working on a floor plan for a new office space. She has sketched a quadrilateral tile pattern ABCD with sides AB = 5 feet, BC = 7 feet, CD = 6 feet, and DA = 8 feet. To create a smaller version of this tile for a sample display, she decides to scale down the design from a fixed reference point O using a scale factor of 0.5, producing the reduced tile pattern PQRS.

When she orders material for the side QR of the sample tile, which corresponds to side BC of the original, how many feet of material should she request?
