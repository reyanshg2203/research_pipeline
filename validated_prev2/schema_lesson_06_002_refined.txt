A designer is creating two similar decorative tile patterns. The first pattern, quadrilateral PQRS, has sides PQ = 4, QR = 6, and SP = 5. The second pattern, quadrilateral WXYZ, has sides WX = 6, XY = 9, YZ = 12, and ZW = 7.5.

The designer confirms that quadrilateral PQRS is similar to quadrilateral WXYZ using Side-Side-Side similarity, with a scale factor of 1.5 from PQRS to WXYZ.

To complete the first tile pattern, the designer needs to find the length of the remaining side RS in quadrilateral PQRS. What should this length be?
