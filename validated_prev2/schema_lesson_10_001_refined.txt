A designer is working with two similar triangular templates. The first template, triangle PQR, has sides measuring PQ = 9 cm, QR = 12 cm, and PR = 15 cm. She needs to create a smaller version, triangle XYZ, by scaling down the original using a scale factor of 1.5.

She has already cut two sides of the smaller template: XY = 6 cm and YZ = 8 cm. Before she finishes cutting the third side, she wants to verify her work and determine the correct length for XZ.

Using the Side-Side-Side Triangle Similarity Theorem, find the length of XZ.
