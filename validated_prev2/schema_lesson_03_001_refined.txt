A graphic designer is working on a logo that features triangle ABC with sides AB = 5 cm, BC = 7 cm, and AC = 8 cm. To create a smaller version of the logo for use on business cards, she scales down the design from a fixed point P using a scale factor of 0.6, producing the reduced triangle DEF.

When she prepares the artwork for printing, she needs to know the length of side DE on the business card version. What should that length be?
