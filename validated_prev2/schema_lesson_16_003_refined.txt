A surveyor is mapping two similar right triangles formed by sight lines across a ravine. In the smaller triangle JKL, the two legs measure JK = 8 meters and KL = 6 meters, with the right angle at K. The larger triangle MNO is similar to triangle JKL by AA similarity, with the corresponding angles L and O being congruent. The surveyor knows that one leg of the larger triangle, MN, measures 12 meters.

Using the proportional relationships between the similar triangles, what is the length of side NO in triangle MNO?
