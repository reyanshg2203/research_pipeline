A quilter is designing two similar quilt panels. The smaller panel is quadrilateral PQRS with sides PQ = 4 cm, QR = 6 cm, RS = 8 cm, and SP = 5 cm. The larger panel is quadrilateral WXYZ with sides WX = 6 cm, XY = 9 cm, and ZW = 7.5 cm. The quadrilaterals are similar, and all pairs of corresponding angles are congruent.

(a) What is the scale factor of the dilation that takes quadrilateral PQRS onto quadrilateral WXYZ?

(b) Find the length of side YZ in quadrilateral WXYZ.

(c) Describe a sequence of rigid motions and a dilation that could take quadrilateral PQRS onto quadrilateral WXYZ. Be sure to specify the scale factor you use in your dilation.
