A graphic designer is creating a projected image of a company logo. The designer places a projector at point O and projects a small segment EF from the original logo onto a wall. The projector enlarges all distances from O by a scale factor of 2.5. The projected image segment GH on the wall measures 15 cm long.

To verify the setup, the designer measures the distance from the projector at O to point G (the image of E) and finds it to be 7.5 cm.

(a) Find the length of the original segment EF.

(b) Based on the definition of dilation, what is the distance from O to point E (the original location before enlargement)? Explain your reasoning in terms of how a dilation scales distances from the center.
