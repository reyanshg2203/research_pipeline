Two triangular garden plots are being designed for a community landscape project. The first plot, JKL, has side lengths JK = 8 feet, KL = 10 feet, and JL = 12 feet. The second plot, DEF, has side lengths DE = 12 feet, EF = 15 feet, and DF = 18 feet.

A landscape designer needs to know whether the two plots have the same shape so she can adapt her sprinkler layout from one plot to the other. 

(a) Prove that triangles JKL and DEF are similar using the Side-Side-Side Triangle Similarity Theorem. In your proof, explain how a dilation followed by a sequence of rigid motions (or a congruence theorem) shows the triangles must be similar.

(b) Determine which angle in triangle DEF corresponds to angle L in triangle JKL. Explain your reasoning by identifying how the vertices correspond based on the side ratios.

(c) Use the Law of Cosines to find the measure of angle L in triangle JKL. Then state the measure of the corresponding angle in triangle DEF.
