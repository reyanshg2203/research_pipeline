The designer will dilate this triangle with a scale factor of 0.6, using point P as the center of dilation, to create the new logo triangle A'B'C'. Point P is located 10 cm from vertex A.

(a) What is the perimeter of the new scaled-down logo?

(b) How far is point P from vertex A' after the dilation?
