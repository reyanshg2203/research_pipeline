Two landscape architects are designing triangular garden plots. The first garden plot is triangle MNO with sides MN = 10 feet, NO = 14 feet, and MO = 12 feet. A nearby school wants a larger version of this garden using the same shape but with NO = 21 feet. The larger garden is triangle PQR, where the sides correspond as follows: side MN corresponds to side PQ, side NO corresponds to side QR, and side MO corresponds to side PR.

(a) Write a similarity statement for the two triangles.

(b) What is the scale factor of the dilation that takes triangle MNO onto triangle PQR?

(c) Find the length of side PQ in the larger garden plot.

(d) Describe a sequence of rigid motions and a dilation that would map triangle MNO onto triangle PQR. Explain why your sequence works.
