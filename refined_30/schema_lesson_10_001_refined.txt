Two triangular truss braces are used in a construction project. The larger brace, PQR, has sides PQ = 10 cm, QR = 14 cm, and PR = 18 cm. A smaller brace, XYZ, is designed to be a scaled copy of the larger brace, with sides XY = 15 cm and YZ = 21 cm.

First, identify the scale factor by finding the ratio of corresponding sides for the two pairs of sides you know. Then use this scale factor to find the length of XZ.

Finally, verify that all three pairs of corresponding sides have the same ratio, confirming that triangle XYZ is similar to triangle PQR by the Side-Side-Side Similarity Theorem.
