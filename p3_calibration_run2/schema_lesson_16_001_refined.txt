A construction crew is building two similar reinforced concrete roof supports shaped like right triangles. The larger support, triangle PQR, has legs measuring PQ = 9 feet and QR = 12 feet, meeting at a right angle at Q. The smaller support, triangle XYZ, is scaled down from PQR and also has a right angle at Y. The crew knows that the side XY on the smaller support measures 6 feet.

Since both supports have the same interior angles (they sum to 180°), and since the right angles at Q and Y match, the two triangles are similar by the AA Similarity Theorem.

Using the proportional relationships between corresponding sides of these similar triangles, what is the length of side YZ on the smaller support?
