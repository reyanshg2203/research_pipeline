A contractor is designing two rectangular floor plans for similar buildings. The first building, ABCD, has dimensions AB = 4 meters, CD = 4 meters, and DA = 6 meters, with all four corners being right angles. The second building, PQRS, has dimensions PQ = 6 meters, QR = 9 meters, RS = 6 meters, and SP = 9 meters, also with all four corners being right angles.

The contractor confirms that quadrilateral ABCD is similar to quadrilateral PQRS by the SAS similarity criterion, with a scale factor of 1.5. The corresponding angles are congruent: ∠A ≅ ∠P, ∠B ≅ ∠Q, ∠C ≅ ∠R, and ∠D ≅ ∠S.

To complete the floor plan for building ABCD, what is the length of side BC?
